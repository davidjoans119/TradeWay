TRADEWOOD — HOKA CHALLENGER 8 PRODUCT STORE

Run:
- Extract the ZIP.
- Open index.html in a modern browser, or deploy the folder to Netlify/Vercel.
- Keep assets/ beside index.html.

Included:
- Responsive mobile + desktop storefront.
- User-provided HOKA Challenger 8 Jade / Truffle Salt product photo.
- Mouse drag on laptop and finger drag on mobile to tilt the shoe in a 3D perspective.
- Product cards, cart drawer, and demo checkout UI.

IMPORTANT 360-DEGREE LIMITATION:
The available user photo and the product page's accessible primary product image are single-angle photographs. The current interaction is a 3D perspective tilt, not a true 360-degree spin. A real 360 viewer needs either:
1) a real GLB/GLTF 3D model of this exact shoe, or
2) a complete set of product photos captured at evenly spaced angles (ideally 24–36 frames).
Do not market this prototype as a genuine 360 viewer until those assets are added.

The displayed price is based on the referenced listing and can change. This is a demo storefront only: no live inventory, payment gateway, shipping integration, customer accounts, or order backend.
